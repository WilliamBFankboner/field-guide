Thought and Action

The emphasis on reflection, as opposed to action, in McLuhan's works is echoed in Christian quietism, which enjoins passive meditation and the suppression of will, and in Eastern religions like Buddhism in which meditation leads to a state of inactivity and enlightenment, or Nirvana. But, while these religions are often referred to as passive, they are in fact actively engaged with the world on an intellectual level. Meditation requires physical and emotional serenity, a condition that propagates itself: grappling with the mystery of existence requires deep concentration and one of its products is further concentration. Knowledge of the universe and the human condition stills the soul and fulfills man's deepest yearning for understanding, i.e. it is a metaphysical end in itself. Do-gooders and empire-builders need not apply.
