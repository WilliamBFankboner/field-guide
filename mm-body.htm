The Mystical Body

What is the difference between the human eye and the extensions of the eye, like corrective lenses, a cataract lens replacement or electric lighting? For that matter, what is the difference between human consciousness before and after the inventions of the telegraph and satellite broadcasting? The answer is that there is no discernable functional boundary between the mind and the extensions of the mind. The extensions of consciousness in electric media, such as the telegraph and satellite communication, what McLuhan calls the 'electric surround,' or the 'mystical body,' constitute mind-stuff. When we click the remote control to view our favorite TV program, it is no different than accessing a memory or generating a thought. The TV is no longer a mere extension of consciousness, it is functionally inseparable and indistinguishable from consciousness, and clicking the remote is essentially the same as opening our eyes on awaking from sleep. We become what we behold.
